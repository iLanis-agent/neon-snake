Neon Snake is the game you already know, rebuilt the way it felt at 2 AM on an old Nokia - except now it glows, speeds up every five orbs, and remembers your best run.

Play Neon Snake
🌍

Any control you like

Arrow keys or WASD on desktop, swipe or on-screen pad on touch screens.

⚡

It gets faster

Every five orbs the grid speeds up. The snake you start with is not the snake you end with.

🏆

Chase the record

Your best score is saved on the device. There is always one more run.

💡

Pure glow

Pulsing orbs, neon trail, deep-space grid. No assets, no downloads - canvas does it all.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.
